What it does

Built for the moment you hit a ticker you don't recognise.

🔎 Reads where you read

Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, MarketWatch, CNBC, Reuters, Bloomberg, Seeking Alpha — plus Reddit and X. Cashtags become live, hoverable prices.

🤖 A straight answer

Sector, risk, trend, what the company actually does, the key risks, and a Buy / Hold / Sell verdict in plain language.

👔 Wall-Street consensus

Real analyst buy/hold/sell counts — including foreign and TSX listings most free tools skip. Says plainly when there's no coverage.

📅 Earnings & dividends

Next report date, EPS estimate, dividend yield and ex-date — flagged early when a report is less than a week away.

🔥 Reddit hype meter

Your data stays yours

The short version, and you can check every line of it in the source.

  • Nothing leaves your browser but the ticker you ask about. Watchlist, portfolio, history and chats live in chrome.storage.local, on your machine.
  • Page content is never collected. The content script reads visible text only to spot cashtags, and sends none of it anywhere.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Open source, no build step. 150 KB of plain JavaScript — what's on GitHub is exactly what ships.
